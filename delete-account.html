Delete your account and data for Pixel Fishing: Cozy Adventure

Developer: Amanyu Studio (Muhammad Muchlis Abimanyu), Indonesia · Contact: amanyu851@gmail.com

Option 1: inside the game (instant)

  1. Open the game and go to Settings.
  2. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT and confirm.

This deletes, at once: your anonymous server account, your player code and player name, your gifts and messages, the copy of your progress stored on the server, and the link to your Google account (if you linked one). It also erases all progress, the name and the date of birth stored on your device, so the game starts from the beginning.

Option 2: you no longer have the game installed

Send an e-mail to amanyu851@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Pixel Fishing account. Please include your player ID (shown in Settings → Info, format PD-XXXXXXX) or, if you linked Google, the Google e-mail address you used. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: everything listed in option 1 that is stored on our server.
  • Not stored by us at all: your date of birth is never sent to our server.
  • Kept: e-mails you sent us through Report a Problem, until you ask us to delete them (write to the address above from the same address you used). Data processed by Google (Google Play, Google Sign-In) is deleted through Google's own tools.

Hapus akun dan data Pixel Fishing: Cozy Adventure

Pengembang: Amanyu Studio (Muhammad Muchlis Abimanyu), Indonesia · Kontak: amanyu851@gmail.com

Cara 1: di dalam game (langsung)

  1. Buka game, masuk ke Pengaturan.
  2. Ketuk HAPUS AKUN lalu konfirmasi.

Seketika terhapus: akun anonim di server, kode pemain dan nama pemain, hadiah dan pesan, salinan progres di server, dan tautan akun Google (bila ada). Seluruh progres, nama, dan tanggal lahir di perangkatmu juga dihapus, sehingga game mulai dari awal.

Cara 2: game sudah tidak terpasang

Kirim email ke amanyu851@gmail.com dengan subjek Hapus akun Pixel Fishing saya. Sertakan ID pemain (ada di Pengaturan → Info, bentuknya PD-XXXXXXX) atau, bila kamu menautkan Google, alamat email Google yang dipakai. Akun dihapus paling lama 30 hari dan kami membalas setelah selesai. Kami bisa meminta konfirmasi bahwa akun itu milikmu.

Yang dihapus dan yang disimpan

  • Dihapus: semua yang tersebut pada cara 1 yang tersimpan di server kami.
  • Tidak pernah kami simpan: tanggal lahirmu tidak pernah dikirim ke server.
  • Disimpan: email yang kamu kirim lewat Lapor Masalah, sampai kamu meminta kami menghapusnya (tulis ke alamat di atas dari alamat yang sama). Data yang diproses Google (Google Play, Google Sign-In) dihapus lewat alat Google sendiri.
